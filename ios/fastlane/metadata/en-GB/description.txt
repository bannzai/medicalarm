Medicalarm is a medication management app designed to eliminate the anxiety of forgetting to take your medicine. It is designed with the capability to manage multiple individuals, making it especially useful for those who need to manage medication for someone else, such as children.

■ The following features are offered by Medicalarm:
- Register multiple medications and receive push notifications at the time of taking them.
- Multiple users can be registered as well.
- Keep a record of medication taken, allowing you to look back on when you have taken your medicine.
- If you miss the scheduled medication time and have not recorded it, you will receive a follow-up notification to prevent forgetting to record it.
- We also provide a powerful notification function that delivers notifications even in silent mode.

We encourage you to use this app to prevent forgetting your medication and enjoy a worry-free life.
