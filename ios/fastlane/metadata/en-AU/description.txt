Medicalarm is a medication management app designed to help you remember to take your medication. It is specifically designed to manage multiple individuals, making it particularly convenient for those who need to oversee medication for someone else, such as children.

■ Medicalarm offers the following features:
- Register multiple medications and receive push notifications when it's time to take them.
- Register multiple users for medication management.
- Keep a record of medication intake, allowing you to track when medications were taken.
- Receive follow-up notifications to prevent forgetting to log when you miss a dose.
- A notification system that can work even in silent mode.

Utilise this app to help you remember to take your medication.
