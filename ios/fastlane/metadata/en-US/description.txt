Medicalarm is a medication management app designed to eliminate the anxiety of forgetting to take your medication. It is designed with the premise of managing multiple individuals, making it especially convenient for those who need to manage medication for someone else, such as children.

■ Medicalarm offers the following features:
- Register multiple medications and receive push notifications at the time of taking them.
- You can register multiple users for medication management.
- Keep a medication record, allowing you to review when you took your medication.
- Receive follow-up notifications to prevent forgetting to log medication if you miss the scheduled time.
- A powerful notification feature that delivers alerts even in silent mode.

We encourage you to use this app to prevent forgetting to take your medication and to lead a more secure life.
