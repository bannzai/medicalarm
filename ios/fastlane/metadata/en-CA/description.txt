Medicalarm is a medication management app designed to eliminate the anxiety of forgetting to take your medicine. It is designed with the capability to manage multiple individuals, making it particularly useful for parents or caregivers who need to manage someone else's medication. 

■ Features offered by Medicalarm:
- Register multiple medications and receive push notifications when it's time to take them.
- Register multiple users for medication management.
- Keep a medication log that allows you to reflect on when medications were taken.
- Receive follow-up notifications to prevent forgetting to log when a dose is missed.
- A robust notification system that delivers alerts even in silent mode. 

We encourage you to make use of this app to prevent medication forgetfulness and enjoy a worry-free life.
