Medicalarm is a medication management app designed to alleviate the anxiety of forgetting to take your medication. It is particularly useful for managing the medication of multiple individuals, making it an ideal choice for those who need to oversee someone else's medication, such as parents with children.

■ Features of Medicalarm:
- Multiple medications can be registered, and push notifications will be sent to you at the specified times for taking medications.
- Multiple users can be registered, allowing for easy management of different individuals’ medications.
- You can maintain a medication record, enabling you to reflect on when you took your medications.
- If you miss the time for medication and forget to log it, you will receive follow-up notifications to prevent recording lapses.
- Our powerful notification feature ensures that alerts reach you even in silent mode.

We encourage you to utilize this app to prevent forgetfulness in taking medications and to enjoy a more worry-free life.
